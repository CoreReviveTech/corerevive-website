Laptop motherboard fault assessment

What can go wrong on a laptop board?

We investigate the symptoms, inspect affected circuits and determine whether a practical repair route is available for your laptop model.

💧

Liquid Damage & Spills

Assessment of boards exposed to water or other liquids, including corrosion, damaged traces and affected components. The extent of damage determines repair feasibility.

🔌

No-Power / Dead Laptop

Fault finding when a laptop shows no signs of life, will not start or shuts down unexpectedly. Power input, charging circuits and related components are checked.

🔥

Short Circuits & Burnt Components

Investigation of shorted power rails, burnt circuits and failed components, including capacitors and MOSFETs where accessible and repairable.

⚡

Charging IC & Power Faults

Assessment of charging problems, damaged charging ICs, DC input circuitry and power-management components. The charger, battery and port are also considered as possible causes.

💾

BIOS & Firmware Corruption

Investigation of corrupted BIOS or firmware that may prevent startup or cause boot problems. Reprogramming or chip replacement may be considered where supported.

🧩

CPU Socket & Board Connections

Assessment of damaged solder joints, connection faults and CPU-related board issues. Repair options depend on the laptop's design and whether the component is serviceable.

🔬

Chip-Level Component Repairs

Where technically feasible, failed onboard components such as MOSFETs, capacitors, power controllers and other small components may be replaced.

🔍

Microscope-Assisted Inspection

Fine-pitch board areas and solder joints can be inspected using suitable magnification and diagnostic equipment to help locate faults accurately.

🧪

Post-Repair Testing

After an approved repair, the laptop is checked for stable power behaviour, charging and basic operation. Testing scope depends on the fault and available hardware.

Not every laptop motherboard can be repaired. Extensive corrosion, damaged internal layers, unavailable components or uneconomical repairs may make replacement the better option. We explain the assessment before you decide.

Questions & answers

Laptop motherboard repair FAQs

What to expect before arranging a laptop motherboard assessment.

Which laptops do you repair?

We assess supported laptop makes and models, including Windows laptops and selected MacBook models. Repair feasibility depends on the exact model, board design, parts availability and fault.

How much does it cost to repair a laptop motherboard?

It depends on the laptop model, fault, components required and complexity of the repair. We assess the board and provide a quotation for approval before any repair work begins.

How long does a laptop motherboard repair take?

Timeframes vary depending on diagnostic requirements, repair complexity and component availability. We provide an estimated timeframe after assessment.

Can I send my laptop via courier?

Courier is not a standard collection option. Personal collection by CoreRevive may be requested after 4:00pm, subject to your location and availability. Please request it when booking and wait for confirmation before handover.

Is repairing my laptop motherboard worth it?

That depends on the laptop model, extent of damage, replacement cost and overall value of the device. We explain the findings and practical options so you can make an informed decision before approving the work.

Will you be able to repair liquid damage?

Possibly, but it depends on how far the liquid has spread, whether corrosion has damaged board layers and which components are affected. An assessment is needed before confirming feasibility.

Are laptop motherboard repairs covered by a warranty?

Qualifying repairs include a six-month warranty on parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable service and warranty terms. Coverage for the proposed repair will be explained with your quotation.

No Fix, No Fee labour guarantee

If the agreed fault cannot be successfully repaired, the labour charge for that repair is not payable. Any parts or other costs specifically agreed in advance are handled separately.

Six-month warranty on qualifying repairs

Qualifying motherboard repairs are backed by a six-month warranty on parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable warranty terms and exclusions.
