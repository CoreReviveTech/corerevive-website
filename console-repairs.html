Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about console repairs

Helpful information before you book a PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo repair.

Which consoles do you repair?

We accept assessment bookings for PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo consoles. Send us the exact model so we can confirm the appropriate assessment and repair options.

Do you accept consoles that are still under warranty?

If your console is still covered by the manufacturer's warranty, we recommend contacting the manufacturer or an authorised service provider first. Opening or repairing a console independently may affect its existing warranty. Please check your warranty terms before booking an independent assessment.

How much does it cost to repair my console?

Every console repair starts with a booked assessment. The cost depends on the console model, the cause of the fault, parts required and repair complexity. We provide a quotation for your approval before starting any repair work.

How long does a console repair take?

A typical estimate is 3–5 business days after assessment and approval. The actual turnaround depends on the fault, parts availability and repair complexity. We will advise you if a longer period is expected.

Can CoreRevive collect my console?

Personal collection may be arranged on request after 4:00pm, subject to your location and availability. Please book an assessment and wait for CoreRevive to confirm the collection time before handing over your console.

Is repairing my console worth it?

It depends on the console's age and condition, the fault and the cost of the repair compared with replacement. We can assess the problem and provide a quote so you can make an informed decision before approving any work.

Are console repairs covered by a warranty?

Console repairs are backed by a six-month warranty on qualifying parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable warranty terms. The coverage and exclusions will be explained with your repair quote.

What happens if my console cannot be repaired?

Our No Fix, No Fee guarantee means you only pay labour if the agreed repair is successful. Any parts or agreed collection charges are discussed separately and remain subject to the service terms.
