Device compatibility

Upgrades for the system you own

We work with a range of laptop and desktop brands. Upgrade options vary by exact model and configuration.

💻 Windows Laptops

Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer, ASUS, MSI and other models. Some laptops have soldered memory or limited internal upgrade options.

🖥️ Desktop PCs

Office desktops, custom-built PCs and gaming systems. Desktop upgrade options depend on motherboard, power supply, case and cooling support.

 MacBook & Apple

Selected MacBook models can be assessed. Many newer models have integrated or soldered components, which can limit or prevent internal upgrades.

Please send the exact make, model and (where possible) current component specifications. We confirm compatibility and parts availability before recommending or quoting an upgrade.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Helpful information before booking your upgrade assessment.

Which devices can you upgrade?

We accept assessment requests for many Windows laptops, desktop PCs and selected MacBook models. The exact upgrade options depend on your device model, internal design and supported components.

How much do upgrades cost?

Costs depend on the component, its specifications, availability and installation requirements. We assess your system and provide a quotation for approval before work begins.

How long do upgrades take?

Timing depends on the upgrade type, device design and parts availability. We will provide an estimated timeframe after assessment.

Can I send my device via courier?

Courier is not a standard collection option. Personal collection may be arranged after 4:00pm, subject to your location and availability. Please request it when booking and wait for confirmation before handover.

Is upgrading worth it?

It depends on your computer's age, condition, supported upgrades and what you need it to do. We can explain the likely benefits and costs, and give honest advice if replacement may be a better option.

Will an upgrade make my computer faster?

A suitable upgrade can improve certain tasks, but the results depend on the existing hardware, software and workload. We will explain realistic expectations before you approve the work.

Can I supply my own parts?

Please discuss this when booking. We need to verify the exact part's compatibility, condition and suitability before agreeing to install it. Warranty coverage may differ for customer-supplied components.

Are upgrades covered by a warranty?

Qualifying upgrades include a six-month warranty on parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable service and warranty terms. Coverage for customer-supplied parts, if accepted, will be clarified beforehand.
