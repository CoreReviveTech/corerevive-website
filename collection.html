Simple, clear steps

How personal collection works

Collection is arranged individually. A repair is never started without your approval of the quotation.

STEP 01

Book an assessment

Message us with your device type, make, model, fault and suburb. Ask for personal collection when you book.

STEP 02

Confirm arrangements

We check the location and availability. Collection may be arranged after 4:00pm; wait for our confirmation before handover.

STEP 03

Assessment & quotation

We assess the device and explain the findings, available options and costs. No repair work begins until you approve.

STEP 04

Repair, testing & return

If approved, the agreed work is completed and the device is tested. We then contact you to arrange return or handover.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Not sure whether collection can be arranged for your area? Contact us before making plans.

Which areas in Gauteng do you cover?

Collection is considered across Gauteng, subject to your exact suburb, distance, scheduling and availability. Send us your suburb or area so we can confirm whether arrangements are possible.

Is courier pickup available?

Standard courier pickup and delivery is not currently offered as a regular service. Personal collection may be arranged after 4:00pm by prior request, subject to location and availability.

How much does collection cost?

Any applicable collection or return charge will be discussed and confirmed with you before arrangements are finalised. Do not assume collection is free.

Can any device be collected?

Collection may be possible for laptops, desktops, gaming consoles, GPUs and ASIC miners. Size, weight, condition and location can affect whether collection can be arranged.

How long will the assessment and repair take?

Timing depends on the device, fault, parts availability and the work required. We will provide an estimated timeframe after assessment; no fixed turnaround is guaranteed before diagnosis.

Will you repair my device as soon as it is collected?

No. Collection is for assessment and does not authorise repair work. We first assess the device, provide a quotation and proceed only after you approve it.

What if I do not approve the quotation?

We will discuss the return or handover arrangements with you. Any agreed collection or return charges remain payable where applicable, even if you decline the repair.

Are repairs covered by a warranty?

Qualifying repairs are covered by a six-month warranty on applicable parts and workmanship, subject to the relevant warranty terms and exclusions. The warranty does not make collection or transport charges free.
