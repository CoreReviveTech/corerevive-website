What if we can't complete the service?

If the device is unsupported, too heavily damaged, incompatible with the available software, or the requested procedure is unsuccessful, we will explain the outcome and arrange return of your device.

You will not be charged the full service price for an unsuccessful service. An assessment or diagnostic fee may apply where this is explained and agreed beforehand.

Important data information

Data recovery and extraction are attempt-based services. Successful recovery cannot be guaranteed. Screen unlocking, system repair or reinstall procedures may erase existing data. Where possible, back up important information before the device is submitted.

We will explain known risks before proceeding and will not start chargeable work without your approval.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

How long will the service take?

Timing depends on the device model, fault, software compatibility and the type of service. Some jobs may be completed relatively quickly, while more complex faults or recovery attempts can take longer. We provide an estimate after assessment.

Do I need proof of ownership?

Proof of ownership or authorisation may be required for FRP and screen-lock related services. Please have proof of purchase, account details or other relevant documentation available where possible.

Will I lose my data?

Possibly. Some screen-lock procedures, system repairs and reinstalls can erase data. We will explain the known risks before starting. Data recovery and extraction are separate attempt-based services and are not guaranteed.

What happens if you can't complete the service?

We explain why the service could not be completed and arrange return of your device. You will not be charged the full service price for an unsuccessful service. An assessment or diagnostic fee may apply only where disclosed and agreed beforehand.

Can you recover all my deleted or lost data?

No. Recovery depends on whether the data remains accessible and has not been overwritten, encrypted or made inaccessible by hardware damage. We make an attempt but cannot promise that all or any files will be recovered.

Can you work on any Android phone or tablet?

No. Support depends on the exact make, model, Android version, security patch level, device condition and service required. Contact us with the model number so we can check compatibility before booking.
