Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about desktop repairs

Helpful information before booking your desktop assessment.

What types of desktops do you repair?

We accept assessment bookings for gaming PCs, office desktops, custom-built computers and prebuilt systems, including selected HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer and ASUS models. Send us the make and model so we can check your specific system.

How much does it cost to repair a desktop?

The cost depends on the fault, components required and repair complexity. Every desktop repair starts with a booked assessment. We provide a quotation for your approval before any repair work begins.

How long does a desktop repair take?

A typical estimate is 3–5 business days after assessment and approval. The actual turnaround depends on the fault, parts availability and repair complexity. We will advise you if a longer period is expected.

Do you repair desktops that are still under warranty?

If your desktop is still covered by a manufacturer or retailer warranty, we recommend contacting them first. Independent repairs may affect existing warranty coverage, so please check the terms before proceeding.

Can CoreRevive collect my desktop?

Personal collection may be arranged on request after 4:00pm, subject to your location and availability. Please request it when booking your assessment and wait for CoreRevive to confirm the collection time before handover.

Is it worth repairing my desktop?

That depends on the computer's age and condition, the fault and the repair cost compared with replacement. We can assess the problem and provide a quotation so you can make an informed decision.

Are desktop repairs covered by a warranty?

Qualifying repairs include a six-month warranty on parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable service and warranty terms. Coverage and exclusions will be explained with your quotation.

What happens if my desktop cannot be repaired?

Our No Fix, No Fee labour guarantee means you only pay labour if the agreed repair is successful. Parts and any agreed collection charges are separate and discussed beforehand.
