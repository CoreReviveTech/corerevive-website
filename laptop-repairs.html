Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions about laptop repairs

Helpful information before booking your laptop assessment.

What types of laptops do you repair?

We accept assessment requests for Windows laptops, gaming laptops and selected MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models. We also work with brands such as Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer, ASUS and MSI. Send us the exact model so we can check compatibility and parts support.

How much does it cost to repair my laptop?

The cost depends on the fault, laptop model, parts required and repair complexity. Every repair starts with a booked assessment. We provide a quotation for your approval before any repair work begins.

How long does a laptop repair take?

Turnaround depends on the fault, assessment findings and parts availability. We will provide an estimated timeframe with the quotation or once the repair requirements are confirmed.

Do you repair laptops that are still under warranty?

If your laptop is still covered by a manufacturer or retailer warranty, we recommend contacting them first. Independent repairs may affect existing warranty coverage, so please check the terms before proceeding.

Can I send my laptop via courier?

Courier is not a standard collection option. Personal collection may be arranged after 4:00pm, subject to your location and availability. Please request it when booking your assessment and wait for CoreRevive to confirm the collection time before handover.

Are laptop repairs covered by a warranty?

Qualifying repairs include a six-month warranty on parts and workmanship, subject to the applicable service and warranty terms. Coverage and exclusions will be explained with your quotation.

What happens if my laptop cannot be repaired?

Our No Fix, No Fee labour guarantee means you only pay labour if the agreed repair is successful. Parts and any agreed collection charges are separate and discussed beforehand.

Will my files be safe during an operating system reinstall?

An operating system reinstall can affect stored data. Tell us before assessment if you need files preserved. We will discuss available options first, but data preservation or recovery cannot be guaranteed.
